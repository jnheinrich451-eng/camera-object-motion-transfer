Recover, Retarget, Guide: A Training-Free Framework for Object and Camera Motion Transfer

Honours thesis, Australian National University, 2026

John Zeng — supervisor: Dr Jing Zhang

Code

Given one reference video and one text prompt, the pipeline recovers the reference's camera and subject motion in 3-D, synthesises the target's first frame from the reference's layout and the prompt, and replays the motion in that scene by correcting a frozen video model (Wan 2.1 I2V) at sixteen of its fifty sampling steps. No weights are trained. Results below are the ones reported in the thesis.

Limitations

How the subject moves its own body is not transferred yet: a walking person keeps the model's gait, and a rigid object can be drawn twice. Water inside the guided region can freeze unless excluded from the mask. A reference whose camera fails our checks is refused rather than guessed (the coastal road clip above).
